Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how the Pawzle Clash app (the "app") handles your information. Pawzle Clash is published under the Bewe Studio brand by BRAIN WASH, SAS, 53 rue de Patay, 75013 Paris, France, RCS Paris 790 688 998 ("we", "us").

In short

  • All offline play (Story, Arcade, Duo, Survival) works without sending anything over the internet.
  • Only online play ("Vs a player") and Friends use the internet. There is no account to create: no email, no password, no real name.
  • We do not sell any data, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Information kept on your device

The app saves the following on your device:

  • the player name you choose (it can be anything, it does not need to be your real name);
  • your progress, unlocked content and statistics;
  • your settings (language, music, sound effects, vibrations, controls).

This information does not leave your device, except your player name when you play online (see below). You can erase your progress at any time with "Erase my progress" in the app's Options, or erase everything by uninstalling the app.

Online play

The app only connects to our servers once you choose to play online or to open Friends: to find you an opponent and to pass the game between your two phones. After that, while the app is open, it connects so that your friends can see you are around and challenge you or send you an emote; "Erase my online data" stops this. We then use:

  • an anonymous technical ID, created automatically the first time you play online. It contains neither your name, nor your phone number, nor your email address; it is only used to recognize your app from one game to the next and to keep your player name unique;
  • your player name and chosen character, visible to your opponent;
  • your friends, if you add any: your friend code, your friends list, the players you block, the friend requests and challenges you send or receive, and whether the app is open, visible to your friends with your player name and favorite character;
  • reports, if you report a player: their ID, their player name, your ID and the date;
  • the course of the game (your pieces, your grid, your powers, the emotes you send), sent live to your opponent;
  • the quality of your connection, measured before and during the game, to warn you if it is unstable;
  • your IP address and technical information (app version, device type), seen by our servers as with any internet connection, and kept in logs for security and to keep the service running.

There is no written chat between players: only ready-made emotes. Player names are filtered to keep out offensive words; we may change a player name that breaks this rule. You can report a player (offensive name, cheating, bad behavior) from your friends or at the end of an online game: we review every report, and the reported player does not know who reported them.

When no opponent is available, you may play against an opponent run by the app.

Why, and on what basis

  • To let you play online, as you request (performance of the service you use).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent cheating and abuse, and fix failures (our legitimate interest).

We do no profiling, no targeted advertising and no analytics with this data.

How long

  • The course of a game is erased from our servers at the end of the game, and at the latest 24 hours later.
  • The anonymous ID and its player name are erased after 12 months without online play, or as soon as you ask.
  • A friend request is erased as soon as it is accepted or declined, otherwise with the ID; a challenge as soon as it is answered (10 minutes at most), an emote as soon as it is received.
  • Your friends list is kept until you remove the friend, then erased with the ID.
  • A report is erased once reviewed, and at the latest 3 months later; the reports you made are also erased with the ID.
  • Technical logs are kept for 30 days at most.

Who has access

  • Your opponent sees your player name, your character and the course of the game.
  • Your friends see your player name, your favorite character and whether the app is open.
  • Our servers are provided by Google (Firebase service), which acts on our behalf and may not use this data for its own purposes. The data is hosted in the European Union. Google may process some technical data outside the European Union, in particular in the United States, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses, EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).
  • We do not sell or rent any data.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only for online play, Friends and to check for updates.
  • Vibration: used for in-game effects. You can turn vibrations off in Options.

Updates

To know whether a new version exists, the app asks the Play Store installed on the phone. No personal data is sent.

Children

Pawzle Clash is meant for players aged 13 and over. Online play asks for no identity information and does not allow free chat with other players.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your data, or object to its processing. The simplest way: "Erase my online data" in the app's Options, which erases your ID, your online player name, your friends and everything linked to them. You can also write to support@bewestudio.com, giving your player name. You may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Changes

If we change this policy (for example to add advertising, a leaderboard or sign-in with a Google account), we will update it before the version concerned is released, with the new date at the top of this page.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, write to support@bewestudio.com.
