Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how the Pawzle Clash app (the "app") handles your information. Pawzle Clash is published under the Bewe Studio brand by Brainwash, SAS, 53 rue de Patay, 75013 Paris, France, RCS Paris 790 688 998 ("we", "us").

In short

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Information kept on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • the player name you choose (it can be anything, it does not need to be your real name);
  • your game progress, unlocked content and statistics;
  • your settings (language, music, sound effects, vibrations).

This information never leaves your device and we have no access to it. You can erase your progress at any time with "Erase my progress" in the app's Options, or erase everything by uninstalling the app.

Fonts

The current version of the app downloads its fonts from Google Fonts when it starts. As with any web request, your device's IP address is then visible to Google, under Google's privacy policy. The app sends nothing else to Google.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only for the fonts mentioned above.
  • Vibration: used for in-game effects. You can turn vibrations off in Options.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that use personal data (for example online play), we will update this policy before releasing it and show the new date at the top of this page.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, write to support@bewestudio.com.
